Use cards more readily for artifacts that support ongoing work: a detailed plan, a checklist that
will grow, a decision with its reasons, or the state of a long job that the human needs to watch.
Read-and-forget answers, routine progress, and completion reports still belong in the thread. Give a
complete answer there even when cards hold supporting detail. Revise an existing card by its key as
the work changes, and split artifacts only when each is useful to keep.

This file allows more detail in useful artifacts; it does not turn every reply into a card. The board
conventions decide what belongs on a card and which form fits it.
